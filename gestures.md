## Everyday gestures

| What you do | What happens |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Tap** the corner around the search button | Search |
| **Swipe** the search button left / right | Jump one page |
| **Press and hold** the search button | A short menu: **Pages & wallpaper**, **Dextera settings**, **Android settings**. Keep holding, slide to one, and let go |
| **Hold and slide sideways** along the search button | Move through all your pages; lift to stay on one |
| **Swipe up** on the search button (opened fold) | Shows **Switch positions** to swap two linked pages |
| **Tap a page icon** beside the search button | Jump straight to that page |
| **Tap a folder** | Its first apps fan out right beside your thumb; tap one to open it. Tap the folder again to open all of it |
| **Hold a folder and slide** | Fan out its apps and slide to one; let go to open it |
| **Drag the folder row** sideways | Bring a far folder to your thumb, with a light tick as each one passes |
| **Tap All apps** | Tuck your folders inside it; tap again to bring them back |
| **Drag the All apps tile** up or down | Move your page up or down — see [Moving your page](#moving-your-page) |
| **Scroll down** a list | The date and folders slide away so more fits; they come back at the top, with Back, or after a few seconds |
| **Slide along the letter rail** | Jump the app list (or contacts rail, if enabled) |
| **Long-press** an app or contact | Open the quick menu (keep holding when it lights up) |
| **Hold** a notification's picture | Pin that app's notifications to the top |
| **Pinch in** or **long-press empty space** | Page overview |
| **System back** | Close menus and search first |
| **Hold back** (if on) | Jump to the page you chose in settings |
| **Swipe sideways low on the screen** (opened fold) | Moves the search button to the other half |
| **Double-tap** any bare part of the wallpaper (if set) | Runs your chosen action |
| **Home** | Returns to Dextera — see [Home button](#home-button) |

## Settings for gestures

  <img class="shot" src="{{ '/assets/images/wiki_gestures.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Touch and Gestures settings" width="320" />

**Settings → Touch & Gestures:**

| Setting | Use it for |
|---------|------------|
| **Left-handed mode** | Search button and alphabet rail on the **left** for easier thumb reach |
| **Swipe the search button** / **Swipe the line** / **Swipe to move the search button** | Turn off any swipe you keep triggering by accident |
| **Home screen gestures** | Master switch for double-tap / swipe shortcuts |
| **Long-hold back gesture** | Hold back briefly to jump to a page |
| **Double-tap wallpaper** | e.g. Toggle left-handed |
| **Hold back gesture** | Target page (e.g. Notifications) |
| **Home button & gesture** | Default, **First page**, or jump to a page |

## Home button {#home-button}

### First page (recommended if you jump around a lot)

1. You’re in another app → **Home** → back to **the page you left**.  
2. **Home again** while already on Dextera → **first page**.  

## Moving through pages

- Quick swipe on the search button = one page  
- Hold and slide sideways along the search button: your pages follow it; a large name helps you aim  
- Run out of room? Slide back the other way to keep going  
- Lift your finger to stay on that page  

## Moving your page {#moving-your-page}

- With folders shown as icons, the **All apps** tile has a small bar under it. **Drag the tile** up or down to move everything below it. A tap still tucks your folders away.  
- Otherwise, **press and hold the line** under your folders, then drag up or down.  
- **Lock it:** keep holding after the drag and slide sideways toward the edge until you feel a double bump.  
- Each page remembers its own spot.  

## Music page

- On the record or artwork: **swipe down** to play or pause, **swipe left or right** to change songs, **tap** to open the player app.  
- The time played shows around the record, with the song length in the corner.  
- Mini player: it shows how far into the song you are. Tap it for play, pause and skip, or drag its bar to move through the song.  

## Search and back

- Back first closes a long-press menu if one is open  
- Then it closes search / the keyboard and returns you to home content  

## Tips

1. Stay on the search button for most page moving — less reaching.  
2. Double-tap, Home, and hold-back can also open **Settings** or **Appearance**, or reveal items hidden on rest.  
3. Hold the search button, pinch, or long-press empty space when you need overview.  
4. Try **First page** on Home if you want one press for “where I was” and a second for “home base.”  
5. Turn on **left-handed mode** if the letter rail is easier on the other edge.
