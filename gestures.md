## Everyday gestures

| What you do | What happens |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Swipe** the search bar left / right | Jump one page |
| **Press and hold** the search bar, then slide | Move through all your pages; lift to stay on one |
| **Swipe up** on the search bar (opened fold) | Shows **Switch positions** to swap two linked pages |
| **Tap a page dot or icon** under the search bar | Jump straight to that page |
| **Tap the gear** on the search bar | Open **page overview** |
| **Slide along the letter rail** | Jump the app list (or contacts rail, if enabled) |
| **Long-press** an app or contact | Open the quick menu (keep holding when it lights up) |
| **Hold and drag the line** above your page | Move the page up or down — see [Moving your page](#moving-your-page) |
| **Swipe left / right on the small dash** | Next / previous page |
| **Pinch in** or **long-press empty space** | Page overview |
| **System back** | Close menus and search first; on home it **cycles pages** (unless traditional swiping is on) |
| **Hold back** (if on) | Jump to the page you chose in settings |
| **Pull down** past the top of a list | Reachability — your page moves closer to your thumb for a few seconds |
| **Swipe on empty space** (opened fold) | Moves the search bar to the other half |
| **Double-tap** the clock/header (if set) | Runs your chosen action |
| **Home** | Returns to Dextera — see [Home button](#home-button) |
| **Swipe pages** (traditional mode) | Standard side-to-side paging |

**Settings → Touch & Gestures:**

| Setting | Use it for |
|---------|------------|
| **Left-handed mode** | Alphabet rail on the **left** for easier thumb reach |
| **Home screen gestures** | Master switch for double-tap / swipe shortcuts |
| **Long-hold back gesture** | Hold back briefly to jump to a page |
| **Double-tap header** | e.g. Toggle left-handed |
| **Hold back gesture** | Target page (e.g. Notifications) |
| **Home button & gesture** | Default, **First page**, or jump to a page |

## Home button {#home-button}

### First page (recommended if you jump around a lot)

1. You’re in another app → **Home** → back to **the page you left**.  
2. **Home again** while already on Dextera → **first page**.  

## Moving through pages

- Quick swipe on the search bar = one page  
- Press and hold the search bar, then slide your finger: your pages follow it; a large name helps you aim  
- Run out of room? Slide back the other way to keep going  
- Lift your finger to stay on that page  

## Moving your page {#moving-your-page}

- **Press and hold the line**, then drag up or down to move the page.  
- **Lock it:** keep holding after the drag and slide sideways toward the edge until you feel a double bump.  
- **Hide the long line:** swipe it the other way.  
- Left alone, the line folds into a **small dash**. Tap the dash to bring the line back.  

## Music page

- On the record or artwork: **swipe down** to play or pause, **swipe left or right** to change songs, **tap** to open the player app.  
- Mini player: tap left, middle, or right for previous, play/pause, next; slide sideways to move through the song; slide up or down for volume.  

## Search and back

- Back first closes a long-press menu if one is open  
- Then it closes search / the keyboard and returns you to home content  

## Tips

1. Stay on the bottom search bar for most page moving — less reaching.  
2. Double-tap, Home, and hold-back can also open **Settings** or **Appearance**, or reveal items hidden on rest.  
3. Use the **gear**, pinch, or empty long-press when you need overview.  
4. Try **First page** on Home if you want one press for “where I was” and a second for “home base.”  
5. Turn on **left-handed mode** if the letter rail is easier on the other edge.
