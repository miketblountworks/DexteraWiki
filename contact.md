Dextera Labs reads mail at **@dexteralabs.io**. Use the address that matches what you are writing about so it lands in the right pile.

| Address | Use it for |
|---------|------------|
| **[features@dexteralabs.io](mailto:features@dexteralabs.io)** | Ideas and feature requests |
| **[bugs@dexteralabs.io](mailto:bugs@dexteralabs.io)** | Something broken or unexpected |
| **[support@dexteralabs.io](mailto:support@dexteralabs.io)** | Setup help, how-to, “I can’t find X” |
| **[privacy@dexteralabs.io](mailto:privacy@dexteralabs.io)** | Privacy questions and [data deletion]({{ '/data-deletion/' | relative_url }}) |
| **[security@dexteralabs.io](mailto:security@dexteralabs.io)** | Security issues |
| **[hello@dexteralabs.io](mailto:hello@dexteralabs.io)** | Everything else — press, business, or if you are not sure |

The [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dexteralabs.home) contact channel also reaches us.

## What to include

**Feature request** — what you want, why it would help, and how you would use it.

**Bug report** — what you expected, what happened, device and Android version, Dextera version (**Settings → About**), and whether you are on Play, Dev, or a sideload build.

**Support** — what you are trying to do and where you are stuck. Check [Getting started]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}) and [Features]({{ '/features/' | relative_url }}) first if you can.

**Privacy / deletion** — see [Delete data]({{ '/data-deletion/' | relative_url }}). Suggested subject: `Dextera Launcher — data deletion request`.

We aim to reply within **30 days**.
